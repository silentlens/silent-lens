A few answers

Good to know before we begin.

The essentials on timing, revisions, rights, and how a project moves forward.

What does Silent Lens create?

Cinematic AI advertising, short-form performance creatives, AI-generated advertising images, creative concepts and hooks, visual storytelling, campaign variations, and cinematic brand films.

Can international clients work remotely?

Yes. Silent Lens serves international clients remotely. Scope, communication language, schedule, and deliverables are confirmed in writing before production.

How does the 50% deposit work?

A 50% deposit is required before production begins. The remaining 50% is due before final full-resolution deliverables are released.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds are included in the specified packages. One revision round means one consolidated batch of feedback.
