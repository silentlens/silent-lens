Project terms

Simple, explicit.

The workflow is inquiry, written proposal, invoice/payment instructions, deposit, production, revisions, final balance, and delivery. There is no online checkout on this first version.

Payment & revisions

  • A 50% deposit is required before production begins.
  • The remaining 50% is due before final full-resolution deliverables are released.
  • Two revision rounds are included in the specified packages.
  • One revision round means one consolidated batch of feedback.
  • Changes outside the agreed scope or major changes to an approved concept may require a separate quote.
  • Production does not begin before the deposit and required project information are received.
  • Exact deliverables, schedule, and scope are confirmed in writing before production.

Usage rights & project files

After full payment, the client receives commercial usage rights to the agreed final deliverables for the intended uses described in the project agreement, subject to applicable law and the terms governing AI-generated material, third-party assets, music, fonts, and other licensed elements.

  • Original editable project files and working files are not included unless separately agreed and priced.
  • Exclusivity, buyouts, or special licensing requirements must be discussed and documented before production.
  • Silent Lens may feature completed work in its portfolio unless a confidentiality agreement or written arrangement says otherwise.

Note: These website terms are not a substitute for tailored legal advice.

FAQ

Before you brief us.

Answers that match the way Silent Lens actually works today.

What does Silent Lens create?

Cinematic AI advertising, short-form performance ad creatives, AI-generated advertising images, creative concepts, hooks, visual storytelling, campaign variations, and cinematic brand films.

Which brands and industries can work with the studio?

Ambitious brands and startups across categories can get in touch. The right fit depends on the brief, visual needs, timeline, and agreed scope rather than a fixed industry list.

Can international clients work with Silent Lens remotely?

Yes. The studio works remotely with international clients. Communication language can be English, French, or Arabic, subject to the agreed project workflow.

How long does a project take?

Starter Ad projects are estimated at 5–7 business days and Signature Creative at 10–15 business days. Cinematic Video and Campaign Studio timelines depend on confirmed scope. Estimates begin after the deposit and all needed information/assets are received.

What is included in each package?

See the package details above. Packages do not automatically include unlimited revisions, source files, paid advertising management, or guaranteed campaign performance.

How does the 50% deposit work?

A 50% deposit is required before production begins. The remaining 50% is due before final full-resolution deliverables are released. Payment instructions are handled through the written proposal and invoice/payment instructions.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds are included in the specified packages. Each round is one consolidated batch of feedback rather than unlimited ongoing changes.

What happens if the project scope changes?

Changes outside the agreed scope or major changes to an approved concept may require a separate quote. Scope, deliverables, and schedule are confirmed in writing before production.

Do clients receive commercial usage rights?

After full payment, the client receives commercial usage rights to the agreed final deliverables for the intended uses described in the project agreement, subject to applicable law and the terms governing AI-generated material and licensed elements.

Are editable project files included?

Original editable project files and working files are not included unless separately agreed and priced.

Can clients request custom campaigns?

Yes. Campaign Studio is designed for custom campaign concepts, visual direction, multiple videos, advertising images, and creative variations defined in a proposal.

How are AI tools, music, and third-party assets handled?

They are selected and handled subject to the applicable tool terms and licenses. The project agreement should document relevant deliverables, usage, and any special licensing requirements.

How should clients submit their brief and references?

Start with the inquiry form or email. Reference material and project assets can be shared after scope is agreed; file uploads are not required on this first inquiry form.
